FOUNDATIONS OF INTERIOR DESIGN

Design principles for every room

The principles below are not connected to only one specific room. They are general interior design guidelines that can be applied throughout a home to create spaces that feel organized, comfortable, balanced, and visually connected.

01

Balance

Distribute furniture, color, and visual weight throughout a room so that one side does not feel heavier than another.

02

Contrast

Combine differences in color, texture, shape, or material to make important elements stand out.

03

Emphasis

Create a clear focal point, such as artwork, a fireplace, a bed, or another feature that attracts attention.

04

Rhythm

Repeat colors, shapes, patterns, or materials to guide the eye through the room and create visual consistency.

05

Scale

Choose furniture and decorations that fit the size of the room and relate appropriately to one another.

06

Unity

Use related colors, materials, and styles so that all parts of the room feel like they belong together.

07

Function

Make sure the room supports the activities that take place there and meets the practical needs of its users.

08

Flow

Arrange furniture so people can move comfortably through the room without blocked entrances or crowded pathways.
